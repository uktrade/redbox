{{ tool.name }} helps free trade agreement negotiation teams research questions, understand evidence and prepare for negotiations.

It brings together information sources, including trade agreements, economic data, trade statistics, policy research and selected news websites.

To get started and get the most out of the tool:

  • describe the question or topic you want to investigate, including relevant countries, commodities or policy areas
  • be specific about the information you need, such as an overview, a trade value, or what a particular document says about a topic
  • add relevant context by uploading your own documents
  • check the citations in the response to verify the information and confirm that appropriate sources have been used
How the tool works 

The tool will:

  • interpret your question and choose appropriate agents and information sources
  • retrieve relevant information from its available sources
  • answer broad research questions, such as factors affecting trade between countries
  • answer specific questions, such as what a document says about a topic or the value of trade in a commodity
  • provide citations that link to the sources used, allowing you to check and verify information

The tool may show you its proposed plan before producing an answer and ask whether it should proceed.

How to get the most from this tool

You can get the most from this tool by:

  • including relevant details in your question, such as the countries, commodities, documents, or topics you want it to consider
  • asking a focused question when you need a specific piece of evidence
  • uploading documents that provide additional context or contain information you want the tool to examine
  • selecting previously uploaded documents when you want to use them again in a later question
  • following the citations to check the evidence and verify the answer
